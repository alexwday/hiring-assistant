# GG08 Data Workflow Analyst

We are hiring a Data Workflow Analyst to help modernize internal hiring,
document review, and operations workflows. The role combines stakeholder
engagement, practical automation, data analysis, and responsible use of LLMs.

## Responsibilities

- Translate business problems into clear workflow requirements and implementation
  plans.
- Build or prototype internal tools using Python, SQL, APIs, and lightweight web
  interfaces.
- Analyze operational data to identify bottlenecks, quality issues, and hidden
  process risks.
- Create dashboards, markdown documentation, and decision-support summaries for
  managers.
- Work with risk, compliance, and operations stakeholders to ensure tools are
  auditable and human-reviewed where needed.
- Evaluate candidate, client, or document information using consistent criteria
  while surfacing non-obvious strengths and risks.

## Qualifications

- Experience with Python, SQL, Excel, and data visualization tools.
- Strong communication skills with non-technical stakeholders.
- Demonstrated ability to improve manual workflows through automation.
- Familiarity with LLM prompts, document parsing, metadata extraction, or AI
  assisted review is an asset.
- Comfort documenting assumptions, quality checks, and limitations.

## Additional Context

The team values pragmatic builders who can move from ambiguous requirements to
working prototypes, but who also understand controls, explainability, privacy,
and fair decision-making. Candidates without a traditional software background
can be strong fits if they show evidence of execution, clear thinking, and
measurable operational impact.
